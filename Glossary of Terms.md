Glossary of Terms

* AWGN (Additive White Gaussian Noise): A foundational channel noise model used in information theory to simulate thermal noise and broadband degradation in communication links.
* AXI-Stream: A high-speed streaming protocol standard used in FPGA designs for point-to-point data transmission utilizing tvalid, tready, and tdata handshaking signals.
* Convolutional Autoencoder: An unsupervised deep learning network utilizing convolutional and transpose-convolutional layers to compress spatial data into a bottleneck and reconstruct it, leveraged for anomaly detection via high reconstruction error.
* FFT / IFFT (Fast Fourier Transform / Inverse Fast Fourier Transform): Algorithmic transformations used to convert signals between time domains and frequency domains, essential for OFDM modulation and demodulation.
* FPGA (Field-Programmable Gate Array): An integrated circuit configured post-manufacture via hardware description languages like VHDL to implement custom digital logic and hardware accelerators.
* I/Q (In-phase and Quadrature): The real and imaginary components of a complex baseband radio frequency signal utilized extensively in digital communications and software-defined radios.
* Least Squares (LS): A traditional, low-complexity linear estimation technique used in wireless receivers to approximate channel frequency responses from known pilot symbols.
* NMSE (Normalized Mean Square Error): A standardized error metric used to evaluate the accuracy of estimated channel matrices relative to true channel responses.
* OFDM (Orthogonal Frequency-Division Multiplexing): A multi-carrier digital modulation scheme used in high-speed wireless standards like LTE and 5G NR.
* Rayleigh Fading: A statistical fading model used to simulate multi-path signal scattering, reflection, and attenuation in complex wireless propagation environments.
* SDR (Software-Defined Radio): A radio communication architecture where traditional hardware components are replaced or augmented by software-based signal processing.
* SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio): A metric comparing the power of a transmitted signal to the power of background noise, expressed in decibels (dB).
* STFT (Short-Time Fourier Transform): A digital signal processing technique that computes sequential Fourier transforms over windowed time segments to generate 2D time-frequency spectrograms.
* VHDL (VHSIC Hardware Description Language): A hardware description language used to formally model and synthesize digital electronic systems and integrated circuits.
